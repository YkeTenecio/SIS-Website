Research Agendas & Priorities

As a publicly-funded university, one of the mandates of MSU-IIT is to contribute to the national and societal goals through the conduct of interdisciplinary, multidisciplinary, collaborative, and/or independent studies.

The University's Research Agenda are encapsulated in the following broad categories:

  • ICT: Software development for various applications; integration of ICT in teaching; on-line learning systems.
  • Biotechnology: Drug and nutraceutical discovery and development; application of bioinformatics; bioremediation; industrial enzymes; biosafety and ethical implications of biotechnology.
  • Material Science: Engineered bamboo; nanotechnology applications of biomaterials; solid waste recycling; biopolymers.
  • Health and Environment: Cancer surveillance; dengue transmission and control; climate change and health; food nutritive value fortification and safety; ethnobotany and biodiversity.
  • Renewable Energy: Bioenergy production; non-conventional power generation; biodegradation systems for energy production.
  • Peace and Governance: Governance issues in health and environmental management; contextualization of science and technology for the promotion of peace.
  • Gender and Development: Gender equality; GAD sensitivity and awareness; gender empowerment.
  • Disaster Risk Reduction and Management: Disaster geo-informatics; robotics for disaster; hazard assessment.
  • Curricular Innovations and Emerging Pedagogies: Researches that promote inclusive, transformative, innovative and equitable quality education.
  • Economics and Business Education: Business intelligence, enterprise development, livelihood and employment.

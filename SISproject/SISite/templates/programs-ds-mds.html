Overview

The Master of Data Science program is designed to provide students with a structured and comprehensive learning journey over two academic years. It combines foundational knowledge, practical skills, and advanced concepts in a progressive manner, ensuring that students develop the expertise needed to excel in the rapidly evolving field of data science. The curriculum includes a balanced mix of coursework, assessments, and a culminating capstone project that integrates theoretical understanding with real-world application.

Application and Admission

Minimum Requirements

  1. A Bachelor's degree, or Master's degree, from a recognized institution is required for admission to a master's or doctoral program, respectively.
  2. For an applicant to a master's program or to a doctoral degree program, should have an undergraduate Grade-Point-Average (GPA) of 2.00 or better, and for an applicant to a doctoral program, a GPA of 1.75 or better in master's program; or some evidence of potential ability to pursue a graduate degree, e.g., excellent performance in teaching or research, etc.
  3. Evidence of suitable background or its equivalent in the field of study sought.

The above criteria shall be enforced through (a) examination/evaluation of scholastic credentials, (b) recommendation from two former professors and/or distinguished authorities in the field/discipline sought, (c) character reference, (d) personal interview, (e) admission test, and (f) concept paper of the possible research topic or any appropriate means.

Applicants must submit all required credentials to the Program Coordinator/Adviser in the Department at least 2 weeks before enrollment. Credentials are evaluated by the Program Coordinator/Adviser and are endorsed to the college/center for further appraisal and approval.

Application for Sustainable Development Studies Program

Step 1: Submission of the Accomplished Forms to the SDS Program Coordinator

  • 2 copies – Application for Admission (download here)
  • Concept Paper (download here)
  • Personal Statement (download here)
  • 2 Recommendation Letters (download here)
  • Transcript of Records (Original and Photocopy)
  • Certificate of Overall GPA/GWA (Photocopy)
  • Honorable Dismissal (For non MSU-IIT applicants)
  • Transfer Credentials (For non MSU-IIT applicants)
  • 2x2 Picture (2)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
  • Income Tax Return
  • Long Brown Envelope (1)
  • Long Green Expanded Envelope (1)
  • if applicable: Marriage Certificate for Female (Photocopy)

Online Submission: Submit all accomplished forms and documents through this link.

Courier Submission: You may send your documents via courier (e.g. LBC) addressed to:

  • Dr. Ernesto E. Empig
  • SIS Office CASS Old Building, Ground Floor
  • Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology
  • Andres Bonifacio Avenue, Tibanga, Iligan City, 9200

Step 2: The Program Coordinator evaluates the application and interviews the applicant.

Step 3: The student receives a copy of the Notice of Admission.

Step 4: The student downloads, prints, and accomplishes the payment slip for admission, then proceeds to the Institute Cashier for payment.

Step 5: The student submits the required application documents to the Admissions and Registrar's Offices.

Step 6: The student proceeds to the Program Coordinator for enrollment.

Step 7: After receiving the Certificate of Registration (COR), the student proceeds to ICTC (Now CITC) at the CCS Building for school ID processing. The student also registers a MY.IIT account online by following the instructions indicated in the COR.

In the case of a student seeking admission to the graduate program whose English proficiency is low, the student will be advised to satisfy the English proficiency requirement before the student can be admitted as a regular student.

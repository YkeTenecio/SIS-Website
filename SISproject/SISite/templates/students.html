Overview

The School of Interdisciplinary Studies (SIS) brings together a diverse group of students from various disciplines who are united by a shared commitment to sustainability, innovation, and evidence-based problem solving. Learners come from different academic backgrounds and professional sectors within and beyond MSU-IIT, creating a rich environment for peer learning, collaboration, and interdisciplinary dialogue.

Students are actively engaged in coursework, research, and extension activities that address real-world issues such as environmental management, sustainable development, data-driven policy, and community resilience. Their achievements include participation in funded research projects, contribution to environmental impact assessments and community-based initiatives, and involvement in student organizations that promote sustainability advocacy and interdisciplinary engagement.

SIS supports students through mentoring, flexible learning pathways, and access to cross-college electives that allow them to tailor studies to their interests and career goals.

Overview

The School of Interdisciplinary Studies (SIS) at MSU-IIT serves as a hub for integrated education in sustainability, environmental science, and data science.

These programs prepare students to become sustainability experts, environmental stewards, and data innovators.

Core Programs

SIS anchors its offerings on these foundational areas:

  • Sustainable Development Studies
  • Environmental Science
  • Data Science

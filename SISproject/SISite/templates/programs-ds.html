Overview

The Data Science program at MSU-IIT's School of Interdisciplinary Studies blends statistical analysis, computer science, and domain-specific knowledge to equip students with skills for interpreting large datasets and extracting actionable insights. Delivered as a graduate-level offering, it addresses surging industry demand across healthcare, finance, technology, and sustainability sectors, preparing graduates for roles as data scientists, analysts, and engineers who drive evidence-based innovation and decision-making.

The interdisciplinary curriculum integrates computational tools, machine learning, data visualization, and ethical considerations, drawing faculty expertise from MSU-IIT's College of Computer Studies and College of Science and Mathematics to foster real-world applications. Students benefit from flexible delivery modes, cross-disciplinary electives, and research opportunities aligned with SIS priorities in sustainable development and complex problem-solving.

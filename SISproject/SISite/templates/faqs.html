FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

    Who can Apply?

  • Professionals from government, NGOs, academe, and industry are welcome to apply, especially those who want to integrate sustainability into their field.

    • Can I study while working full-time?

  • Yes. The program is designed for professionals with a hybrid setup and flexible scheduling.

    • How much is the tuition fee?

  • The tuition fee is ₱1,500.00 per unit.

    • How long does it take to complete the program?

  • Master’s Degree: 2 to 3 years
  • Doctorate Degree: 3 to 5 years

    • What is the difference between the Academic Track and the Professional Track?

  • Academic Track: Research-oriented, focusing on knowledge production and scholarly work. Prepares students for careers in research and academe.
  • Professional Track: Practice-oriented, emphasizing the application of knowledge in real-world settings. Geared toward policy work, development practice, and organizational leadership.
